Built on the Claude Code you already have.

          
Today: no API keys, no extra account, no browser extension.

        

        

          
01
Uses your own sign-in
Cap runs Claude Code on your Mac and signs in through Claude Code's own browser sign-in. It never sees your password or a token.

          
02
Checks while you work
Every 15 seconds while you're at your Mac, using the smallest model. It pauses when you're away.

          
03
Shows what Claude reports
Whole-percent figures exactly as Claude reports them, with the time left and the reset time. No estimates.

        Vision, mission and roadmap
        
Make the costs of AI visible, so using it responsibly becomes a habit.

      

      
Every prompt runs on real hardware that draws real energy and water. Most of us never see that. Cap starts with the usage numbers Claude already reports, and grows toward helping you understand what sits behind them. The better we understand our usage, the more intentionally, ethically and sustainably we can use these tools.

    

    

      
Vision
Anyone who uses AI can see what it costs, and choose with that in mind.

      
Mission
Give Claude users clear, honest numbers about their usage, then the context to act on them.

      
Ethos
Knowing your usage is the first step to using these tools responsibly. Every estimate Cap shows will come with a method anyone can check.
